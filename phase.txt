3. KEEP USERACCOUNT AND EMPLOYEE SEPARATE
==================================================

Do NOT merge UserAccount and Employee entities.

They represent different concepts.

UserAccount:

- authentication
- login identifier
- password/session
- permissions
- role assignment
- company access
- locale
- notification preferences
- security/access settings

Employee:

- employee code
- legal/display name
- department
- designation
- manager
- joining date
- employment status
- shift
- work location
- attendance policy
- employment metadata

Keep domain separation.

BUT:

My Profile UI should intelligently combine them when linked.
