PHASE 15.1 — EMPLOYEE PROBATION PERIOD, CONFIRMATION WORKFLOW & PROBATION-SPECIFIC LEAVE POLICIES

Continue working on the EXISTING Flutter ERP project and the Leave/Holiday implementation from Phase 15.

DO NOT create a separate Probation module unnecessarily.

Probation belongs primarily to:

Employee Employment Information

and integrates with:

Leave Policies
Leave Eligibility
Employee Profile
HR workflows
Dashboard
Notifications
Reports

IMPORTANT:

Phase 15 previously only included lightweight concepts such as:

probationRestrictionEnabled
minimumServiceDays

That is NOT sufficient.

Implement probation properly as an employment lifecycle concept.

==================================================
1. CORE PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Probation is NOT a Leave Type.

Probation is NOT an Attendance status.

Probation is an EMPLOYMENT STATE/PERIOD.

Leave policy must be able to behave differently:

BEFORE confirmation

DURING probation

AFTER confirmation

Do not hardcode:

"employees on probation get no leave."

Different companies may have different policies.

Everything must be configurable.
